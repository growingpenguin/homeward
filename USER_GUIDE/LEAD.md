# I'm a lead. What do I do?

[All guides](USER_GUIDE.md) · [Volunteer guide](VOLUNTEER.md) · [Guide for the person asking for help](PERSON_LOOKING_FOR_HELP.md)

A lead can do everything a volunteer does. A lead also does three extra jobs:

1. **Decide about children.**
2. **Protect people** who are in danger.
3. **Replace lost slips.**

**How to read this guide**

- Every step has a picture. A **red box** shows where to type or press. The picture shows the screen **after** you have typed or chosen.
- Lost? Press **Home** in the top bar.
- **Bold words** are words you see on the screen.
- You should know the [volunteer guide](VOLUNTEER.md) first.

**You need**

- The test link: https://hilarious-cassata-a51a3c.netlify.app
- Made-up people only. This practice uses **Tamsin Quillfeather** (age 9) and **Bryn Quillfeather**.

> The pictures come from a practice copy. Your slip codes will look different. That is fine.

**Can I add a new volunteer or lead?** Not in this version. There is no button for it. The demo has six ready-made accounts. See [adding staff](USER_GUIDE.md#can-we-add-a-volunteer-or-lead).

## Part 1. A child needs help (you decide)

### Step 1. Log in as a lead

Log in at **Eastside Community Center** as **Lead Morgan**. PIN **2468**.

### Step 2. Press "Lead page: children and safety flags"

Only leads see this button.

![Press the Lead page button.](screenshots/l02-lead-home.png)

### Step 3. Find the child's request

Look under **Children waiting for a lead**.

![The card for Bryn and Tamsin.](screenshots/l03-children-waiting.png)

### Step 4. Check in person

Follow your shelter's safeguarding process. A matching name is **never** enough. The app cannot do this check for you. If you are not sure, **stop**. Do not tick a box only to make the button work.

### Step 5. Tick the two boxes

Tick **I checked the guardian…** only after you checked.
Tick **The person at this desk agreed in person…** only if it is true.
**Both boxes are needed**, every time.

![Both boxes ticked.](screenshots/l04-two-checks.png)

### Step 6. Write a note and the plan

Under **How you checked**, write what you did. Under **How they will meet**, write: **Bryn comes to the Eastside desk at 3 p.m.**

![Type the note and the plan.](screenshots/l05-note-and-plan.png)

### Step 7. Press "Check what will be shared"

The button turns on only when all four things are filled in.

![Press Check what will be shared.](screenshots/l06-check-what-will-be-shared.png)

### Step 8. Read it, then press "Yes, release: connect them"

![Press Yes, release: connect them.](screenshots/l07-release.png)

The request leaves the list. You see **No child is waiting.**

![Done. The request is gone from the list.](screenshots/l08-released.png)

**Check it:** log in at **Westfield** as **Volunteer Casey**. Check **Bryn's** slip ([volunteer guide, Part 4](VOLUNTEER.md#part-4-pell-comes-back-with-his-slip)). Bryn sees the meeting plan.

## Part 2. Protect someone in danger

Use this when someone says they are in danger from a person looking, or you think so.

### Step 1. Open the person's page

On the lead page, under **Children without an adult here**, press **Tamsin Quillfeather**.

![Press the child's name.](screenshots/l09-children-without-an-adult.png)

**An adult?** Use their own slip page right after you register them. If a code and PIN are showing, write them down first. Then press **Fix a mistake**, then **Save changes**. You come back to the page with the lead buttons. The demo has no list of all people yet.

### Step 2. Press "Protect this person: never share anything (lead)"

![Press Protect this person.](screenshots/l10-protect-button.png)

### Step 3. Read the question, then press "Yes, protect them"

![Press Yes, protect them.](screenshots/l11-protect-confirm.png)

You see **Protected. Nothing about them will be shared.**

![Tamsin is protected.](screenshots/l12-protected.png)

### Step 4. See the flag on the lead page

Go home. Press the lead page button. Look under **Safety flags at this shelter**.

![The flag is listed here.](screenshots/l13-safety-flags.png)

**Check it:** at **Westfield**, check **Bryn's** slip again. The meeting plan is gone. Bryn sees the waiting message. Protection also hides Tamsin from every new search.

Protection cannot take back something someone already read or wrote down.

## Part 4. Replace a lost slip

The old slip and PIN **stop working** at once. The new PIN is shown **once**.

### Step 1. Open the person's page

On the lead page press **Tamsin Quillfeather** again.

### Step 2. Press "Reprint a lost slip (lead)"

![Press Reprint a lost slip.](screenshots/l15-reprint-button.png)

### Step 3. Read the warning, then press "Yes, make a new slip"

![Press Yes, make a new slip.](screenshots/l16-reprint-confirm.png)

### Step 4. Print or write down the new code and PIN now

![The new slip. Give it to the right person.](screenshots/l17-new-slip.png)

### Step 5. Check it at Westfield

Log in as **Volunteer Casey**. Press **Check a code**.

- Type Tamsin's **old** code and PIN. You see **That code and PIN didn't match…**

![The old slip no longer works.](screenshots/l18-old-slip-fails.png)

- Type the **new** code and PIN. You see **Slip of Tamsin Quillfeather.**

![The new slip works.](screenshots/l19-new-slip-works.png)

- Press **Finished with this person**. The boxes are empty again.

![Press Finished with this person.](screenshots/l20-finished-with-this-person.png)

**An adult lost their slip and no page is open?** The demo cannot find an adult by name yet. Ask the project maintainer for help. Do not register the person a second time.
