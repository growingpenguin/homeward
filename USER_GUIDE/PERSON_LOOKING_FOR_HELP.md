# I'm looking for someone, or someone may be looking for me

[All guides](USER_GUIDE.md) · [Volunteer guide](VOLUNTEER.md) · [Lead guide](LEAD.md)

**You do not log in.** You do not need a phone. You do not need an ID. Go to the desk of any shelter. A volunteer uses Homeward **for you**. You tell the volunteer what you need. **You decide what is shared.**

> The pictures come from a practice copy with made-up people. Your slip will look different.

---

## 1. "I want my family to know I'm here"

### What you tell the volunteer

- A name or a nickname. It does **not** have to be your real name.
- Your age, if you know it. A guess is fine.
- Nothing else is needed. Share more only if you want to.

### Choose what happens if someone looks for you

The volunteer reads you this question: **If someone comes looking for you, what should we do?** You pick one:

| You say… | What it means |
|---|---|
| **"Ask me first."** | Nobody is told anything until you say yes. This is the usual choice. |
| **"Tell them where I am."** | A volunteer may help family reach you. Safety checks still happen. |
| **"Keep me out of searches."** | Nobody searching can find you. Shelter staff still know you are here. |

![The volunteer reads these out. You choose.](screenshots/v07-privacy.png)

**Are you in danger from someone?** Tell the volunteer **quietly** and ask for a **lead**. You never have to agree to contact.

### You get a slip

The slip has a **code** and a **PIN**. Keep **both**. Keep them private. You need them to check for news, at **any** shelter.

![Your slip. Keep the code and the PIN together.](screenshots/v11-slip.png)

The PIN is shown **only once**. Ask the volunteer to print it or help you write it down.

**Lost your slip?** Ask for a **lead**. A new slip makes the old one stop working.

## 2. "I'm looking for my family"

1. Tell the volunteer your name or nickname.
2. Tell them who you are looking for. Any names or nicknames help. A rough age helps too. "I don't know" is fine.
3. The volunteer may send a request for you. You can give a short message, for example **"Mama, it is Pell. I am safe."** Do **not** say where you are in that message.
4. Take **your own slip**. You get one even if nobody is found.

![The volunteer shows you possible matches. They never show where anyone is.](screenshots/v16-possible-matches.png)

A possible match is a **clue**, not proof. It also does not mean the person said yes.

**Looking for more than one person?** Tell the volunteer. One slip can cover all your searches.

---

## 3. "Is there any news?"

1. Bring your slip to **any** shelter desk.
2. Tell the volunteer the code and the PIN. Say them quietly.
3. The volunteer turns the screen towards you. **Read it yourself.**

You may see one of three answers:

**"They want to connect. Here's how: …"** The other person said yes. The screen shows the meeting plan. Ask the volunteer to help you follow it.

![They want to connect.](screenshots/v34-connected-answer.png)

**"They asked us to tell you they are safe."** They chose to tell you only that. It does not say where they are.

![They are safe.](screenshots/v35-safe-answer.png)

**"Your request is saved. If they choose to connect, we'll let you know here."** There is nothing to share yet. Come back and check again.

![Nothing to share yet.](screenshots/v24-turned-screen-waiting.png)

This waiting message looks the same when someone has not answered, wants more time, or said no. That keeps their choice private and keeps people safe. You are **not** told which one it is.

When you finish, ask the volunteer to give the screen back. They clear your check before the next person.

---

## 4. "Someone is asking about me"

The volunteer reads you the request. You can say:

- **"Yes, I want to connect."** First agree on the exact words or meeting plan.
- **"Tell them I'm safe, but not where."** Only that short message is shared.
- **"Not now."** You can decide later.
- **"No."** They are **not** told that you said no.

If a **child** is involved, a **lead** has to do extra checks before anything is shared.

You can ask staff to change your details or your choice at any time.

---

## Your safety, in short

- Nobody is told where you are unless you say yes.
- The volunteer types for you. You never log in.
- You can say **no** and nobody is told.
- Keep your slip private.
