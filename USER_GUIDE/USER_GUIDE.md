# Homeward user guide

**Homeward** helps shelter volunteers reunite families after a hurricane. Many people have no phone and no ID. Volunteers register people, look for relatives across **all** shelters, and pass messages along.

**Nobody is told where anyone is unless that person says yes.**

**Live link:** https://hilarious-cassata-a51a3c.netlify.app

---

## Pick your guide

Each guide has **a picture for every step**. A red box in the picture shows what to press or type. You do not need to know anything first. Lost? Press **Home** in the top bar of the app.

| I am… | Open this guide | What you will learn |
|---|---|---|
| **A volunteer** at a shelter desk | **[Volunteer guide](VOLUNTEER.md)** | Log in, register someone, search for family, check a slip, answer a request, copy paper forms |
| **A lead** (a volunteer in charge) | **[Lead guide](LEAD.md)** | Decide about children, protect someone in danger, replace a lost slip |
| **Someone who needs help** (looking for family, or someone may be looking for me) | **[Guide for the person asking for help](PERSON_LOOKING_FOR_HELP.md)** | What you choose, what your slip is, what the answers mean |

## Who does what

- **Volunteer.** Works at the desk. Types for people. Reads messages aloud. Presses the button the person chooses.
- **Lead.** A volunteer with extra jobs: children, protection, lost slips.
- **The person at the desk** never logs in. They talk to the volunteer. At one moment the volunteer turns the screen towards them.

## Before you start

- **Waiting for the server.** The first visit can take 30 to 60 seconds. You see **Connecting. Please wait.** Wait. Do not keep reloading.
- **Use a private (incognito) window** to test. Close it when you finish.
- **Use made-up names only.** Never real people.
- **Demo PIN:** **2468** for every login below.

| Shelter | Volunteer | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| Lincoln High Shelter | Volunteer Alex | Lead Jordan |
| Eastside Community Center | Volunteer Riley | Lead Morgan |
| Westfield High Gym | Volunteer Casey | Lead Taylor |

If you see **Too many tries**, someone tested wrong PINs on that login. Wait 15 minutes, or use **another** login from the table.

On the login screen: choose the **shelter**, type the **name**, type the **PIN**, press **Log in**.

## Can we add a volunteer or lead?

**Not through the app yet.** The six logins above are demo accounts made during setup. There is no staff sign-up, invitation page or add-staff button. A lead cannot add volunteers, promote people or reset staff PINs from the app.

For the demo, use the six listed accounts. A new account must be set up outside the app by the project maintainer. Never share a staff PIN.

A sensible next feature: a lead adds volunteers at their own shelter, and a trusted administrator adds leads. That is not built.

## Staying safe on a shared computer

- After **10 minutes** without use, the screen locks. Type your PIN and press **Unlock**.
- When you finish, press **Log out**. Unsaved drafts are cleared.
- The slip PIN disappears when you leave the slip screen. Print it or write it down first.
- After **Give the screen back to the volunteer**, the next person sees nothing from the last check.

## Quick help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Connecting. Please wait.** | The server was asleep. Wait up to a minute. After two minutes, reload once. |
| **That didn't work. Check the shelter, your name and your PIN.** | Check the shelter, type the name exactly as in the table, use PIN 2468. |
| **That code and PIN didn't match.** | Check both against the slip. A wrong code and a wrong PIN give the same message. Five wrong PINs lock that slip for 15 minutes. A lead may also have replaced the slip. |
| **The search found nobody.** | Try another spelling or a nickname, or remove the age. The search is saved. Give the slip. Look again later ([Volunteer, Part 6](VOLUNTEER.md#part-6-look-at-an-old-search-again)). |
| **A child card has no buttons.** | That is correct. Ask a lead. |
| **I made a typo.** | Press **Fix a mistake** on the person's slip page. Never register them again. |
| **I cannot see the Lead page.** | Only leads see it. Log in as a lead. |
| **The screen locked.** | Type your PIN. Press **Unlock**. |
