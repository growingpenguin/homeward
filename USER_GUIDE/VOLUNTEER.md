# I'm a volunteer. How do I use Homeward?

[All guides](USER_GUIDE.md) · [Lead guide](LEAD.md) · [Guide for the person asking for help](PERSON_LOOKING_FOR_HELP.md)

You work at the desk of a shelter. People come to you. You type for them. **They decide what is shared.**

**How to read this guide**

- Every step has a picture.
- A **red box** in the picture shows where to type or what to press. The picture shows the screen **after** you have typed or chosen, so your own screen may start empty.
- **Bold words** are the words you see on the screen.
- Lost? Press **Home** in the top bar. It takes you back to the big buttons. (Write down a slip first. The PIN is shown only once.)
- Do the steps in order. Do not skip ahead.

![The Home button is always in the top bar.](screenshots/v42-home-button.png)

**You need**

- A laptop or tablet with internet.
- The test link: https://hilarious-cassata-a51a3c.netlify.app (the first visit can take up to 1 minute; wait for **Connecting. Please wait** to go away).
- Made-up people only. This practice uses **Odalys Fennimore** and **Pell Ashgrove**.

> The pictures come from a practice copy of Homeward. Your slip codes will look different. That is fine.

## Part 2. Someone arrives at your desk

Odalys has just arrived. She wants her family to be able to ask about her. You will register her.

### Step 1. Press Someone is here

![Press Someone is here.](screenshots/v03-home.png)

### Step 2. Press "No, the person is here with me"

![Press No, the person is here with me.](screenshots/v04-choose-mode.png)

### Step 3. Type the name

Type **Odalys Fennimore** in the box **What should we call you?** Press **Next**.

A nickname is fine. You do not need a real name or an ID.

![Type the name. Press Next.](screenshots/v05-name.png)

### Step 4. Type the age

Type **80**. Press **18 or over**. Press **Next**.

If you do not know the age, leave it empty and press **Not sure**. The person will then be treated as a child, to keep them safe. A lead will decide later. If you know they are a grown-up, press **18 or over**.

![Type the age. Press 18 or over. Press Next.](screenshots/v06-age.png)

### Step 5. Read the question out loud

Read to Odalys: **If someone comes looking for you, what should we do?**
She chooses. Press her choice. For this practice, choose **Ask me first**. Press **Next**.

![Press the choice she says. Then Next.](screenshots/v07-privacy.png)

| If she says… | Press… |
|---|---|
| "Come and ask me first." | **Ask me first** |
| "You may tell them how to reach me." | **Tell them where I am** |
| "Do not let anyone find me." | **Keep me out of searches** |

### Step 6. Skip the extra boxes

You do not need them. Press **Next**.

![Press Next.](screenshots/v08-extras.png)

### Step 7. Check and press Save

![Press Save.](screenshots/v09-check-and-save.png)

You see **Odalys Fennimore is saved.**

### Step 8. Press Print a slip

![Press Print a slip.](screenshots/v10-saved.png)

### Step 9. Give her the slip

The slip has a **Code** and a **PIN**. **The PIN is shown only once.** Press **Print**, or write both down now. Give it to Odalys. Tell her: "Any shelter can use this to check for news."

![The slip. Your numbers will be different.](screenshots/v11-slip.png)

**Made a mistake?** Press **Fix a mistake**, change it, then press **Save changes**. Her slip stays the same. Never register her again to fix a typo.

![Press Fix a mistake.](screenshots/v12-fix-a-mistake.png)

You see **Possible matches**. The card says why it might be the person. **It never shows where anyone is.**

![Press Ask this person.](screenshots/v16-possible-matches.png)

Type: **Mama, it is Pell. I am safe.** Never write where Pell is. Press **Send the request**.

![Type the message. Press Send the request.](screenshots/v17-write-message.png)

![Press Done: print their slip.](screenshots/v18-asked.png)

![Press Look for someone else too.](screenshots/v19-look-for-someone-else.png)

Press **Print a slip**. Then press **Print**, or write down **Pell's code and PIN**. Give him the slip.

![Pell's slip. Keep it private.](screenshots/v20-pell-slip.png)

**Nobody found?** Still give Pell his slip. The search is saved. You cannot tell why nobody appeared. That is on purpose.

## Part 6. Look at an old search again

People may register after a search was made. To look again:

1. Go to the shelter where the search was made.
2. Press **Check a code**. Type the slip code and PIN. Press **Check**.
3. Press **Their search for …** (it shows the names that were searched).

![Press the search.](screenshots/v26-their-search.png)

4. Press **Check again for new matches**.

![Press Check again for new matches.](screenshots/v27-check-again.png)

If someone new matches, a **new card** appears. If the same cards stay, nobody new has been registered yet. Come back later.

## Part 7. Copying a paper form

Use this when you copy forms from paper.

### Step 1. Press "Yes, I'm copying paper forms"

You find it after **Someone is here**.

![Press Yes, I'm copying paper forms.](screenshots/v36-paper-mode.png)

### Step 2. Write where the form is, and its date

Type **Sheet 4, line 12** in the first box. Then type the date in **Date written on the paper**. The box shows month, day, year: for practice type **10/20/2026**. If a real form has no date, use the day the forms were collected. Press **Next**. (This is **Step 1 of 6**. That is normal for paper forms.)

![Type the sheet, the date. Press Next.](screenshots/v37-paper-sheet-and-date.png)

### Step 3. Type the person, as in Part 2

For practice: **Sora Willowbright**, age **42**, **18 or over**, **Ask me first**. Save. Print the slip.

### Step 4. Press Next person

![Press Next person.](screenshots/v38-next-person.png)

The **date stays filled in**. The sheet box is empty, ready for the next form.

![The date stays. The sheet box is empty.](screenshots/v39-date-kept.png)

To go back to normal registration, press **Back** on this first paper step.

## Part 8. When you must get a lead

If a **child** is involved, the card has **no buttons**. It says: **A child is involved, so a lead decides. Please ask a lead.**

**Stop. Get a lead.** Do not change the age to get past this.

![This card needs a lead.](screenshots/c01-child-needs-a-lead.png)

To **see** a child card, do the practice in the [lead guide, Part 0](LEAD.md#part-0-practice-setup-this-is-volunteer-work).

Also get a lead if someone may be in danger, or if a slip is lost.

## When you finish

- Press **Log out**. Unsaved drafts are cleared.
- After 10 minutes without use the screen locks. Type your PIN and press **Unlock**.
- Never leave a slip or a person's page open on a shared computer.
